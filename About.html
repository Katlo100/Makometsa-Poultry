Who We Are

Makometsa Poultry is a proudly local poultry enterprise rooted in Gabane, Botswana. We deliver high‑quality poultry products while promoting food security, youth empowerment, and sustainable agriculture.

Our Mission

To supply premium poultry products while empowering communities and practicing ethical farming.

Our Vision

To become Botswana’s most trusted poultry supplier through quality, innovation, and sustainability.
